Privacy policy

Calorie to Kilojoules

An offline converter for food calories (kcal) and kilojoules (kJ).

About this policy

Vasser Studios develops Calorie to Kilojoules, a utility that converts food calories (kcal) and kilojoules (kJ). This policy explains how the app handles information and how to contact us about privacy.

Information in the app

The app performs conversions on your device. Numbers you enter are held in memory to display the conversion. They are not sent to Vasser Studios or any third party, and the app does not save a conversion history.

You can use Clear to reset the visible fields. Values held in memory are discarded when the app process ends. The app works offline and does not require an account.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking services. It does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts or health records.

It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or health data. Information you type into the converter stays on your device.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and resolve support or privacy questions. Please only include information needed for your enquiry.

Our email provider, Microsoft, processes this correspondence to deliver and store email. We do not sell your information or use support messages for advertising. We may disclose correspondence if required by law.

Retention and deletion

There is no conversion history or account information stored by the app for us to delete. You can clear the visible inputs within the app.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. We may need to confirm that a request comes from the person concerned.

Security

Conversion values remain on your device. We take reasonable steps to protect correspondence you send us, but no method of transmission or storage provides an absolute security guarantee.

This policy website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Vasser Studios does not add analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking scripts to this policy website. GitHub’s hosting of this website is separate from the offline operation of the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or our information practices change. The updated date on this page identifies the latest revision.

Privacy enquiries

For questions or requests about this policy, contact Vasser Studios at vasserstudios@hotmail.com.
